OVERVIEW

The Project

Chameleons vs Rhinos is a fast-paced asymmetric multiplayer game designed around two very different player roles.

Chameleons play as thieves attempting to infiltrate a museum, steal valuable artifacts and escape. Rhinos act as guards and must patrol the museum, chase thieves and prevent the robbery.

The game was designed to be easy to understand, fast to play and readable for a younger target audience.

TECHNICAL

Multiplayer Systems

Multiplayer was one of the main technical challenges of the project.

The game uses Godot's multiplayer API together with WebRTC. Gameplay actions are communicated through RPCs, while important game-state decisions are controlled by the host.

04

GAMEPLAY

Asymmetric Roles

Chameleons

The chameleons are quick thieves built around stealth and movement.

  • Third-person perspective
  • Camouflage while stationary
  • Artifact stealing
  • Escape-focused gameplay

Rhinos

The rhinos are guards designed around control, chasing and preventing the robbery.

  • First-person perspective
  • Taser mechanic
  • Capture and jail system
  • Defensive gameplay

05

ITERATION

Playtesting & Iteration

Playtesting was used to identify where players became confused and where gameplay needed clearer feedback.

Because the game was designed for players aged 9–12, readability was especially important.

We focused on reducing unnecessary complexity, improving tutorial communication and making interactions easier to recognize without requiring players to remember large amounts of information.

06

REFLECTION

What I Learned

The project gave me practical experience building systems that needed to work for multiple players rather than only one local player.

I became more comfortable working with RPCs, multiplayer authority, player spawning and synchronization.

It also reinforced how important player feedback, readability and iteration are when building a game for a specific audience.
