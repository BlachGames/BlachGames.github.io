OVERVIEW

The Project

ChronoBlade explores how slowing time can turn fast combat into a tactical decision-making mechanic.

During combat, the player can enter a focused state where time slows down and incoming blades can be analyzed and redirected.

The project focuses heavily on responsive controls, readability and making combat actions feel deliberate and satisfying.

02

MY WORK

Gameplay Programming

My work focused on building and refining the game's core combat mechanics.

Time Manipulation

Built mechanics that slow the game during specific combat states while preserving responsive player input.

Blade Redirection

Developed systems allowing the player to intercept and redirect incoming blades toward new targets.

Targeting

Worked on selecting targets and communicating where redirected attacks would travel.

Combat Feedback

Used visual, audio and camera feedback to make successful actions easier to read and more satisfying.

COMBAT

Blade Redirection

Redirecting attacks transforms enemy projectiles into tools the player can use against other enemies.

When a blade enters the player's control, targeting and prediction systems help communicate its new path.

This mechanic encourages the player to use positioning and enemy attacks as part of their own offensive strategy.

05

GAME FEEL

Making Combat Feel Responsive

A large part of the project was making successful combat actions feel powerful without sacrificing clarity.

Timing, visual effects, sound, camera feedback and clear targeting indicators all contribute to how responsive the mechanic feels.

I repeatedly adjusted these systems while testing how quickly players could understand the result of their actions.

06

REFLECTION

What I Learned

ChronoBlade helped me focus on the small details that make gameplay systems feel responsive and satisfying.

Building the core mechanic required more than simply making the blade change direction.

Targeting, timing, animation, feedback and readability all needed to work together for the mechanic to feel natural to the player.
